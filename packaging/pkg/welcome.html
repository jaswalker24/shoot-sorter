Shoot Sorter

Tags, ranks and culls photo shoots on this Mac and writes keywords to XMP sidecars for Lightroom Classic. Photos never leave the Mac.

  • Requires an Apple-silicon Mac and macOS 14 or newer.
  • Installs Shoot Sorter.app (about 1.3 GB). Choose Install for me only to put it in your home Applications folder without an administrator password.
  • On first launch the app downloads its models (about 7 GB) into ~/Library/Application Support/Shoot Sorter. Keep the Mac awake and online.
  • The app is not signed with an Apple Developer ID. If macOS blocks it, right-click the app in Finder and choose Open (see INSTALL.md inside the app).

Nothing starts at login unless you turn on Start at Login in the menu-bar icon.
